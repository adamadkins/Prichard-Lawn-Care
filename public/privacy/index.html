Privacy Policy

Information about using the Prichard Lawn Care & Tree Service website.

Last updated October 19, 2026. Draft website policy: business owner should review before public use.

Information you provide

When you prepare a quote inquiry or employment application, you may enter your name, contact details, service interests, and information you choose to include. On this version of the website, these forms open a draft in your email application. Information is not submitted to Prichard Lawn Care through the website itself. Sending an email is a separate action you control.

How information may be used

If you contact the business by phone, email, or Facebook, information you share may be used to respond to your request, discuss services, or review your application. Do not include sensitive information in the website fields.

Payments

Online payment links open SwipeSimple, an external payment service. Payment information is entered and processed on SwipeSimple, not on this website. Review their applicable terms and privacy practices before paying.

Third-party services

Links to Facebook and SwipeSimple are external sites with their own practices. Google Fonts and externally hosted images, where used, may cause your browser to request resources from third-party servers. Basic hosting and security logs may also be processed by our web-hosting provider.

Cookies

This website does not currently implement an account system, personalized advertising cookies, or its own analytics tracking scripts. Third-party services may use their own technologies when visited.

Changes and questions

These practices may change as the site evolves. For questions or privacy requests, contact the business at 606-324-9316 or through the contact page.
